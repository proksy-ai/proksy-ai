# Proksy Intelligence Labs

> Building proxy technologies that let individuals maximize their output.

We believe in the intersectionality of human and digital consciousness. As society
evolves, AI will increasingly act as a **proxy** for humans — getting more done than
we ever have. We think this plays out in two ways at once: a massive increase in
digital product output, and a massive transformation of post-industrial, white-collar
job roles.

We're optimists about where that leads. The capability AI hasn't learned — the
**recursive loop** of reflecting, adapting, and reinventing how we work — is the one
humans keep returning to. It may learn it in time; until then, and after, we adapt.
Just as programming moved from low-level to high-level languages, we expect it to move
toward **natural language and neuro signals** — the proxy layer for building your
**digital twin**.

## What we build

We ship both **open source projects** and **proprietary platforms**, all aimed at the
same thing: tools that let individuals get more out of themselves.

- **Open source** — public, community-driven, MIT/Apache-2.0 licensed. Built in the
  open with contributors welcome.
- **Proprietary** — the platforms and products that carry our brands forward.

Browse the repositories below to see what's active.

## Principles

- **Human–AI harmony.** We're progressive about how people and AI collaborate, and we
  build for partnership, not replacement.
- **Economical AI.** We use AI efficiently — no tokenmaxxing for its own sake. Good
  economics is a design constraint, not an afterthought.
- **Build in the open where we can.** Open source first for the foundations; proprietary
  where it earns its keep.
